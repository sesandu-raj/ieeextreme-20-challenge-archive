# Welcome to IEEEXtreme 20.0

Welcome to the 20th IEEEXtreme programming contest! Volunteers have spent months preparing the event, and we are glad to have you take part.

This year's slogan is **“20 Years of Coding Beyond Limits.”** The contest celebrates two decades of empowering coding enthusiasts to compete alongside more than 19,400 peers from 82 countries.

## What to expect

IEEEXtreme runs for 24 hours. Challenges are released gradually throughout the contest; the first two appear five minutes after the contest begins. Check the announcements page for updates.

This edition introduces two challenge categories:

- **AI allowed:** AI tools may be used, subject to the challenge instructions and contest rules.
- **AI prohibited:** AI tools may not be used.

Check each challenge's AI-use label before working on it. Using AI on a challenge where it is prohibited may result in disqualification. Follow the [official IEEEXtreme rules](https://ieeextreme.org/) to help ensure a fair contest.

## Working on challenges

Read each challenge carefully, especially its **Constraints and notes** section. That section may describe partial-scoring data distributions. You can earn partial credit by solving only some of a challenge's test files, so consider attempting every problem even if you cannot find a fully optimal solution.

If you have a question about a challenge or think it contains an error, submit a clarification request through the CSA Q&A system. The technical team will be available throughout the contest. They can answer questions, but they cannot review or comment on your code; this protects the integrity and fairness of the competition.

## Editors and AI use

Most challenges are marked **Locked editor**. For these challenges:

- Write your code in the contest editor.
- Only text produced in your own workspace may be pasted into the editor.
- The editor records how your solution is written.
- AI use is not allowed.

The **game challenges** use an unlocked editor and allow AI use. Always check the individual challenge's label and instructions. The introductory task below also uses the locked editor, so you can try it out before the other challenges appear. Select its **Locked editor** label in the challenge list for details about what is recorded and how to bring in code written before the contest.

## Introductory task: send the team a message

Write a program that prints a message you would like to share with the IEEEXtreme team.

**Input:** No standard input.

**Output:** A text message of no more than 200 words.

**Scoring:** This task is optional and is not scored. The task uses the locked editor.

The team would love to hear from you, so please take a moment to send a message!

## Python performance note

If you use Python 2 or Python 3, the contest introduction recommends submitting with PyPy 2 or PyPy 3. PyPy can run faster than the regular Python interpreter and may help avoid a time-limit failure for an algorithm with an expected time complexity.

## Take care and enjoy the contest

IEEEXtreme can be intense. Get plenty of rest, stay hydrated, and remember to eat. A strong scoreboard result can bring prizes and bragging rights, but having an enjoyable experience can leave you with lifelong memories.

Thank you to all the volunteers, sponsors, and partners who made IEEEXtreme 20.0 possible. Good luck, and have fun!
